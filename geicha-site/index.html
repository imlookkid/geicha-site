Four movements to a perfect bowl

点前

篩う

Sift

Pass 2 grams — one heaped chashaku scoop — through a fine sieve into a warmed bowl. Sifting is the difference between silk and clumps.

注ぐ

Pour

Add 70 ml of water at 75°C. Boiling water scalds the leaf and draws out bitterness; cooler water lets the sweetness through.

点てる

Whisk

Whisk briskly in a loose “M” motion with a bamboo chasen for 15 seconds, keeping the tines just off the bottom of the bowl.

味わう

Drink

When a fine, even foam covers the surface, it is ready. Drink within minutes — fresh matcha waits for no one.

First flush only. Nothing else.

一番茶

Every Geicha tea is made exclusively from the first flush — the very first leaves of spring, when the plant is at its sweetest and most tender after a winter of rest.

We source from Japan's great tea regions: deep umami ceremonial matcha from Uji and Kyoto, gentle and nutty profiles from Kagoshima, and refined floral notes from Shizuoka. Each tea carries the character of its origin — which is why we let you taste a 10 g sampler before committing to a full pouch.
